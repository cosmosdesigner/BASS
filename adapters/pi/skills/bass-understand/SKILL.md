# BASS Understand

Use the repository's evidence-first understanding workflow.

1. Read `AGENTS.md`, `README.md`, and the applicable files under `rules/`.
2. Identify the requested local artifact or evidence item before reading broadly.
3. Explain only claims supported by cited local sources.
4. Classify material records as Fact, Inference, Assumption, Proposal, Question, Conflict, or Decision.
5. Preserve missing evidence and competing sources explicitly; do not resolve conflicts by inference.
6. End with one safe next action and cite the source paths used.
